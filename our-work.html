The Portal Puzzle: is it Right for College?

This white paper is the most comprehensive study ever undertaken to examine the portal and how it’s used. We studied over 800 FBS football portal entrants dating back to the 2020-2021 portal windows. AD Advisors focused on 10 Power 5 schools and five Group of 5 schools and tracked the football career of every student-athlete who used the portal.

NCAA Transfer Portal Hits Record Numbers—Again

With the basketball transfer portal window set to close next week, this White Paper takes a deep dive into over 14,000 basketball student-athletes dating back to 2019. It explores critical metrics including transfer outcomes, competition level changes, how many times the student-athlete entered the portal, and how many teams the student athlete played for.

The study revealed stark realities and patterns that paint a more accurate picture of the portal and its impact.
