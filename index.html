Are You Ready?

To help ADs deal with all the changes, AD Advisors recommend that athletic departments create an equivalent HR Department inside their programs aimed at retaining the student-athletes they have. The “HR Department” needs to focus on retention, benefits, workplace environment, and other matters the private sector has focused on for decades to keep their workforce.
